    🐠 Fish

    Every day. A small pinch of food — they'll come up to the surface for it.

    It's much better to underfeed than overfeed. If in doubt, give less; uneaten food fouls the water.

    The tank light is on a timer, so don't worry if it's on or off at odd hours — that's normal.

    🪴 Plants

    Outdoor plants — every day, unless it has rained. If there's been decent rain, skip it and tick the rain box on that day. Don't water in full midday sun if you can help it; morning or evening is kinder.

    Indoor plants — once a week. I've put these on Sundays so it's easy to remember, but a day either way is absolutely fine.

    💡 Lights

    The lights are automatic and on a timer — the fish tank and the kitchen / living room.

    They'll switch themselves on and off. Nothing needs setting or changing; if they come on when you're not expecting it, that's just the timer doing its job.

    🧳 Going away for a weekend?

    Not a problem at all. There's an automatic fish feeder you can use to cover a couple of days, and it's simpler than it looks:

    1. Open the dispensing hatch as far as the stop. The blu-tack is the stop — that's how far it goes.
    2. Sit it on top of the tank. That's it.

    The machine is set to rotate on a schedule, so there's nothing to set and nothing else to do.

    When you're back, close the hatch and you can lift it straight off.

    Still happy to jump on a call if you'd rather talk it through.

    Just make sure both doors are locked before you head off.
